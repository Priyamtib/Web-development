    The people behind SYNAPSE

    SYNAPSE started with a simple idea: engineers should be as quick with a movie quote as with Kirchhoff's laws. A small team of students from the Dept. of CSE (AIML & CSBS) at IEM Salt Lake planned every question, every circuit and every snack table themselves.

    Our department trains students in machine learning, business systems and software, and we believe the best learning happens outside the classroom, on a stage, with a timer running. This evening is our way of marking IEEE Day.

    Come as a team of friends or come alone. You will leave knowing more than when you arrived.
